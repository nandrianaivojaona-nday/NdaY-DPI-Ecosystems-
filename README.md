# NdaY' DPI Ecosystems 9.2

Official digital gateway of **NdaY' Enterprise**, aligned with **Ecosystem Architecture V9.2** and **TVE Core v3.0.0**. It presents the organisation and its Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) ecosystem to citizens, communities, service providers, businesses, institutions, and partners.

## Positioning

- **NdaY' Ecosystem**: digital infrastructure for economic and social activities (events, beauty, agriculture, tourism, governance, waste).
- **NdaY'Lanona**: Event Operating System built on the ecosystem.
- **Common flow**: Booking (service or asset, quantity/duration) → Payment (method, status) → Notification (event updates, system alerts).

## What Changed from 7.0

- **Architecture 9.2**: independent actor websites, sector-hosted consortium platforms, public territorial discovery, evidence-based citizen progression, and an internal enterprise workspace for NdaY' governance and operations.
- **Shared TVE (v3.0.0)** with shared EbA; sector apps (Lanona, Fako, Tantsaha, Tsidika, Beauty) keep domain ownership only.
- **Separated checks** for authentication, authorization, consent, entitlement, and governance approval.
- **Content** lives in `data/architecture92.ts`; the specification is in `docs/`.
